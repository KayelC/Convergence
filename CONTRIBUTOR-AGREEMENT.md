# Convergence Individual Contributor Copyright Assignment Agreement

**Status:** Review template. Do not use this agreement to accept an external
contribution until the project owner has obtained appropriate legal review and
published a signing process.

This agreement is between **Kayel Calleja** (the **Project Owner**) and the
individual identified in the signature section (the **Contributor**).

## 1. Covered Contributions

A **Contribution** is any source code, test, documentation, data, content, or
other copyrightable material that the Contributor intentionally submits to the
Convergence repository after this agreement takes effect and identifies through
a pull request, commit, patch, or written contribution schedule.

This agreement does not cover material clearly identified in writing as
excluded before submission.

## 2. Copyright Assignment

The Contributor assigns to the Project Owner all worldwide right, title, and
interest in the copyright and copyright-related rights in each Contribution,
including the rights to reproduce, modify, adapt, distribute, publicly display,
publicly perform, sublicense, relicense, commercialize, enforce, and create
derivative works from it for the full duration of those rights.

The Project Owner may license a Contribution under the repository's public
licenses, a commercial license, or other terms, and may change those terms for
future distributions. No royalty or other payment is owed unless a separate
written agreement says otherwise.

## 3. Fallback License And Moral Rights

To the extent that applicable law does not permit assignment of any covered
right, the Contributor grants the Project Owner an exclusive, perpetual,
irrevocable, worldwide, fully paid, royalty-free, transferable, and
sublicensable license to exercise that right, including the right to relicense
it under public or commercial terms.

To the extent permitted by law, the Contributor waives and agrees not to assert
moral rights or similar rights in a Contribution. Where waiver is not permitted,
the Contributor consents to the Project Owner exercising the assigned and
licensed rights without further approval.

## 4. Patent License

The Contributor grants the Project Owner and recipients of Convergence a
perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free patent license for patent
claims controlled by the Contributor that are necessarily infringed by the
Contribution alone or by its combination with Convergence as submitted.

## 5. Contributor Statements

The Contributor states that:

- they have the legal capacity and authority to enter this agreement;
- each Contribution is original to them except for material disclosed and
  approved in writing before submission;
- they have the right to assign and license the Contribution under these terms;
- the Contribution does not knowingly violate another person's copyright,
  patent, trade secret, privacy, publicity, contractual, or other rights;
- no employer, client, school, funder, or other organization owns or restricts
  the Contribution, unless that organization has separately authorized the
  assignment in writing; and
- the Contribution contains no confidential information or malicious code.

## 6. Acceptance And Attribution

The Project Owner is not required to accept, use, distribute, or retain any
Contribution. The Project Owner may edit or remove an accepted Contribution.
Contributor credit may be preserved in version history or project records, but
this agreement does not require a particular form of attribution.

## 7. No Other Relationship

This agreement does not create employment, partnership, agency, payment,
royalty, support, or confidentiality obligations. The Contribution is provided
without warranties except for the Contributor statements above.

## 8. Entire Agreement And Legal Review

This document and any signed contribution schedule form the entire agreement
about the covered Contributions. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both
parties. If a provision is unenforceable, it should be limited or removed only
as necessary without invalidating the remaining provisions.

The governing-law, venue, identity-verification, and electronic-signature
process must be confirmed during legal review before this template becomes an
active contributor agreement.
