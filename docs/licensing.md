# Licensing Convergence

## Intended Model

Convergence is source-available for education, research, personal study,
nonprofit work, and other noncommercial purposes. The copyright owner reserves
commercial licensing.

This is deliberately not described as OSI-approved open source. The
[Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) requires licenses to allow
use in business and other fields of endeavor, while Convergence intentionally
restricts commercial use.

## Repository Scopes

| Material | Public terms |
|---|---|
| Framework, DemoHost, tests, solution, and build files | [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](../LICENSES/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md) |
| Active documentation and original clean content | [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](../LICENSES/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.md) |
| `ArchiveDocs/` | Excluded; historical rights remain with their respective owners |
| Third-party tools and dependencies | Their separately supplied terms |

PolyForm is a software-specific license. Its permitted purposes expressly cover
personal research, experiment, testing, study, hobby projects, charitable
organizations, educational institutions, and public research organizations.
The complete legal text controls where this summary differs.

Creative Commons recommends software-specific licenses for software, while its
licenses remain suitable for documentation and separate creative material. See
the [Creative Commons software guidance](https://creativecommons.org/faq/index.html#can-i-apply-a-creative-commons-license-to-software).

## Examples

Uses intended to be permitted by the public terms include:

- reading the source as part of an academic application or portfolio review;
- studying, testing, or modifying the framework for personal learning;
- using it in coursework or research through an educational institution;
- building a private hobby game with no anticipated commercial application;
- using it for a charitable or other noncommercial organization; and
- sharing permitted modifications with the required license and notices.

Uses requiring a separate commercial agreement include selling a game built
with Convergence or using it to provide a paid commercial product or service.
The legal license text, rather than these examples, determines whether a
particular use is permitted.

## Ownership

Publishing source and granting a license do not transfer ownership. Kayel
Calleja remains the copyright owner of the work he authored and can offer the
same work under separate commercial terms.

An outside contributor normally introduces additional ownership. To preserve a
unified commercial licensor, Convergence will not accept external repository
contributions until the contributor and project owner sign an approved
copyright-assignment agreement. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Earlier Versions

Changing the terms for current and future revisions cannot revoke permissions
already granted for copies distributed under an earlier license. Earlier copies
of Convergence that were released under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 remain usable under
those terms.

## Legal Review

The standard public license texts are retained unmodified. The scope notice,
commercial-licensing process, and contributor-assignment template should be
reviewed by a qualified lawyer before the first commercial agreement or
external contribution is accepted.
