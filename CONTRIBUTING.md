# Contributing To Convergence

Convergence is publicly developed, but accepting source contributions affects
who owns the framework and who can license it. This project therefore separates
discussion from contributions that change repository-owned material.

## Issues And Discussion

Bug reports, design discussions, questions, and feature proposals are welcome.
Do not include confidential information or third-party material that you are
not authorized to share.

## Code, Documentation, And Content Contributions

External pull requests are not accepted by default. Before any external code,
documentation, test, or content contribution can be merged:

1. The project owner must invite or approve the proposed contribution.
2. The contributor and project owner must sign the applicable contributor
   agreement.
3. The contribution must be original or include a complete, compatible rights
   record approved in advance.
4. The contribution must contain no proprietary game data, copyrighted
   franchise material, confidential information, or unapproved dependency.
5. Relevant tests, documentation, formatting, and repository quality gates must
   pass.

The current individual agreement template is
[CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md](CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md). It uses copyright
assignment so the project owner can remain the unified copyright holder and can
continue offering both noncommercial and commercial licenses.

The template must receive appropriate legal review and a signing process before
the first external contribution is accepted. Until then, maintainers will not
merge external contributions that change repository-owned material.

Contributions owned by an employer or another organization require a separate
authorized agreement. The individual template is not sufficient for corporate
contributions.

Submission does not guarantee acceptance. No payment, royalty, employment, or
support obligation is created by proposing or accepting a contribution unless a
separate written agreement says otherwise.
